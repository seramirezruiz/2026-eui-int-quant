Compulsory readings

  1. Bell, A., Fairbrother, M., & Jones, K. (2019). Fixed and random effects models: Making an informed choice. Quality & Quantity 53(2): 1051–1074.

Optional readings

  1. Hazlett, C., & Wainstein, L. (2022). Understanding, choosing, and unifying multilevel and fixed effect approaches. Political Analysis 30(1): 46–65.

  5. Roth, J., Sant'Anna, P., Bilinski, A., & Poe, J. (2023). What's trending in difference-in-differences? Journal of Econometrics 235(2): 2218–2244. (Signpost reading.)
